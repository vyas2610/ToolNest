How to Resize Images for Social Media Platforms

Published September 2026 • 5 min read • Platform Specifications

Every major social media platform enforces strict image aspect ratios and automated recompression filters. If you upload a graphic with the wrong proportions, platforms will forcefully crop off key text, stretch faces, or aggressively compress your visual assets into a blurry mess.

2026 Standard Platform Dimensions

  • Instagram Feed Square: 1080 × 1080 px (1:1 Ratio)
  • Instagram Stories & Reels: 1080 × 1920 px (9:16 Ratio)
  • Twitter / X In-Stream Post: 1200 × 675 px (16:9 Ratio)
  • Facebook Feed Share: 1200 × 630 px (1.91:1 Ratio)
  • YouTube Video Thumbnail: 1280 × 720 px (16:9 Ratio)
  • LinkedIn Post Image: 1200 × 627 px

How to Resize with One Click

Instead of calculating pixel math manually, visit ToolNest's Image Resizer. Open the Social Media Presets dropdown to immediately apply standard Instagram, Twitter, Facebook, or YouTube resolutions with locked proportions.

If your original photo has a drastically different shape, first use our Image Cropper to frame your composition using the 1:1, 4:3, or 16:9 aspect presets.
