About ToolNest PDF Merger

Need to assemble multiple reports, chapters, or legal filings into a cohesive PDF? ToolNest's PDF Merger combines multiple PDF files seamlessly on the client side using PDF-Lib. It copies document catalogues, fonts, and page object streams directly within your browser's memory without sending confidential documents across the web.

How to Merge PDF Documents

  1. Upload two or more PDF files via the upload zone.
  2. Use the up ↑ and down ↓ arrow buttons to arrange the files in your preferred sequence.
  3. Click "Merge PDFs" to stitch the documents together.
  4. Download the final combined document instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Because processing takes place entirely on your device, the only limit is your browser's available RAM. Most computers can comfortably merge dozens of PDFs totaling several hundred megabytes without difficulty.
