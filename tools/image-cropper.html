About ToolNest Image Cropper

The ToolNest Image Cropper offers an intuitive canvas workbench where you can isolate the subject of your photo, adjust aspect ratios, and fix orientation with 90-degree rotations. It renders directly in your browser without transmitting any data over the web.

How to Crop an Image

  1. Drag your image into the dropzone.
  2. Select a preset aspect ratio (like 1:1 for profile pictures or 16:9 for landscape banners) or choose Freeform.
  3. Drag the bounding box or grab its corners to define your frame.
  4. Rotate if necessary, then click "Download Cropped Image".
