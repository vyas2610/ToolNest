About ToolNest PNG to JPG Converter

Standard image converters often turn transparent PNG alpha channels into ugly solid black boxes when saving as JPEG, because JPEG does not support transparency. ToolNest solves this problem by providing an interactive background color picker (defaulting to pure white #FFFFFF), cleanly flattening your PNG before generating the JPG.

How to Convert PNG to JPG

  1. Upload your transparent or opaque PNG file.
  2. Select your desired background fill color for transparent sections.
  3. Adjust the JPG quality slider to balance size and compression.
  4. Click "Download JPG" to save your flattened photo.

Frequently Asked Questions

The JPEG specification does not support alpha (transparency) channels. Basic tools simply discard the alpha byte, which defaults transparent pixels to (0, 0, 0) black. ToolNest fills transparent pixels with your chosen background color (default white) to prevent this issue.
