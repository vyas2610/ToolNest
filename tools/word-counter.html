About ToolNest Word Counter

Whether drafting an essay, preparing an executive summary, writing a blog post, or perfecting social media captions with strict character limits, ToolNest's Word Counter gives you immediate insights into your copy. Every keystroke is calculated on the fly inside your browser without delay.

Calculations & Speed Standards

  • Reading Time: Calculated based on standard adult reading speed of 200 words per minute.
  • Speaking Time: Calculated using the standard public speaking benchmark of 130 words per minute.
  • Character Metrics: Displays both raw character count and character count excluding whitespace to facilitate exact compliance with character-restricted platforms.
