About ToolNest Duplicate Line Remover

Data cleaning is an essential step when processing email subscriber lists, server logs, CSV identifiers, or keyword sets. ToolNest's Duplicate Line Remover strips repeating entries while maintaining the exact initial sequence of your list.

How to Remove Duplicate Lines

  1. Paste your list of items or text lines into the input box.
  2. Configure options such as trimming whitespace, ignoring case, or alphabetical sorting.
  3. The unique list is immediately generated. Copy it to your clipboard or download it as a .txt file.
