About ToolNest Image Resizer

The ToolNest Image Resizer lets you scale images to custom width and height dimensions with sub-pixel precision. Equipped with social media presets for Instagram, Twitter/X, Facebook, and YouTube, this tool guarantees your pictures meet the exact specifications required by digital platforms.

How to Resize an Image Online

  1. Upload Your Image: Drag and drop your image into the dropzone or click to select from your files.
  2. Set Dimensions or Choose a Preset: Enter your desired target width or height. By default, the aspect ratio lock keeps your image proportional so it never looks stretched or squished.
  3. Apply & Download: Click "Apply Resize" to render the resized image on an in-memory canvas, then download your optimized file.
