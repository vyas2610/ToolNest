About ToolNest Case Converter

Whether you accidentally typed a paragraph with Caps Lock on, need to conform headlines to standard editorial Title Case, or need clean lowercase strings, ToolNest's Case Converter does it in one click.

Supported Transformations

  • UPPERCASE: Converts all characters to capital letters.
  • lowercase: Converts all characters to small letters.
  • Title Case: Capitalizes major words while keeping conjunctions and prepositions (like and, in, of, the) lowercase according to standard editorial style guides.
  • Sentence case: Automatically capitalizes the first letter of each sentence following punctuation.
  • Capitalize Words: Capitalizes the first letter of every single word.
  • Alternating Case: Randomizes or alternates casing between capital and small letters.
