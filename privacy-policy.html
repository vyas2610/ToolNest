Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At ToolNest, accessible from https://toolnestonline.netlify.app, privacy is not merely a policy—it is our primary architectural design principle. This Privacy Policy document describes how information is collected, used, and protected when you visit our website.

1. Client-Side Processing Architecture

Unlike conventional web converters that transmit your files to remote cloud servers for server-side processing, ToolNest executes all tool operations directly inside your web browser using client-side technologies (HTML5 Canvas, FileReader, Blob APIs, and client-side JavaScript).

  • Images: When you compress, resize, crop, or convert images, the binary pixels are processed strictly in your local device's memory.
  • PDFs: When you merge PDFs or render pages to JPG, the documents are parsed and modified locally using in-browser libraries (PDF-Lib and PDF.js).
  • Text & Code: When you count words, format JSON, or encode Base64/URLs, the string manipulations happen entirely on your computer.

Your files, documents, and code are never transmitted across the internet to our servers.

2. Information You Voluntarily Provide

Because ToolNest requires no registration, you do not need to provide personal identification information (such as your name, address, or phone number) to access our utilities. If you contact us via our contact form or direct email, we only collect the information you choose to submit (such as your name and email address) solely to reply to your inquiry.

3. Local Storage (Theme Preferences)

ToolNest uses the browser's localStorage API solely to store your light or dark mode theme selection (toolnest_theme). This data is stored strictly on your local browser and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

4. Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

We may use Google Analytics 4 to understand general traffic trends, such as page views, visitor country, and tool usage frequencies.

Critical Privacy Rule: We have configured our tracking architecture to ensure that the contents of uploaded files, text, images, or JSON data are never sent as parameters or dimensions to Google Analytics. Only anonymous UI events (such as "tool_used") are recorded. Google Analytics may place cookies on your device to distinguish unique sessions.

5. Advertising (Google AdSense Readiness)

To keep ToolNest 100% free, we may display third-party advertisements in designated placeholder spaces. Third-party advertising vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this or other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings (google.com/settings/ads).

6. Third-Party CDNs

To maintain fast loading speeds without heavy build artifacts, static libraries (such as PDF-Lib and PDF.js) may be loaded via reliable Content Delivery Networks (such as Cloudflare CDNJS). These CDNs may log standard technical HTTP request headers (such as IP addresses and User-Agent strings) for network security and caching purposes.

7. Data Retention & Security

Because ToolNest does not store your files on any server, there is zero risk of server-side data leaks or unauthorized third-party file access. Once you close or reload your browser tab, all temporary in-memory buffers are immediately cleared by your browser's garbage collection.

8. Contact Information
